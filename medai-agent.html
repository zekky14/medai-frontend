👁️ Eye & Nail Screening

Photo-based screening for signs of jaundice and anemia

ℹ️

This is a screening tool only — based on published research analysing eye and nail bed colour. It cannot replace a blood test. It flags possible signs of jaundice (yellowing, linked to liver/bilirubin issues) and anemia (paleness, linked to low iron/hemoglobin) so you know whether to seek a real lab test.

👁️

How to take this photo

Pull your lower eyelid down gently. In bright, natural daylight (face a window, not the sun), take a close-up photo of the white of your eye and inner eyelid.

💅

How to take this photo

In bright, natural daylight, press your fingernail gently then release. Take a close-up photo of your nail bed and the skin just below your nail, without nail polish.
