👁️ Eye & Nail Screening

Photo-based screening for signs of jaundice and anemia

ℹ️

This is a screening tool only — based on published research analysing eye and nail bed colour. It cannot replace a blood test. It flags possible signs of jaundice (yellowing, linked to liver/bilirubin issues) and anemia (paleness, linked to low iron/hemoglobin) so you know whether to seek a real lab test.

👁️

How to take this photo

Pull your lower eyelid down gently. In bright, natural daylight (face a window, not the sun), take a close-up photo of the white of your eye and inner eyelid.

How to take this photo

Common Questions
Why does the AI ask many questions before diagnosing? ›
This is intentional. Like a real doctor, Krixia follows clinical protocol — gathering sufficient information before forming an assessment. This prevents premature diagnoses and ensures the guidance is relevant to your specific case.
Is my health data safe? ›
Yes. All consultations are protected with AES-256-GCM end-to-end encryption — the same standard used by WhatsApp, Signal, and military institutions. Your data is encrypted on your device before it reaches our servers. We are fully NDPR 2019 compliant and never sell or share your health data.
What AI powers Krixia? ›
Krixia runs a three-tier AI system: Anthropic Claude (primary — highest clinical accuracy), Google Gemini (secondary fallback), and Groq Llama 3 (tertiary backup). This ensures Krixia is always available even if any single provider experiences downtime.
How do I install Krixia as an app? ›
On Android: Open krixia.tickit.com.ng in Chrome, tap the three-dot menu, select "Add to Home screen." On iPhone: Open in Safari, tap the Share button, select "Add to Home Screen." The app works offline once installed.

Recognising a Sickle Cell Crisis
🚨 Vaso-occlusive crisis — Sudden severe pain in bones, joints, chest, or abdomen. Most common type. Seek hospital immediately.
🫁 Acute Chest Syndrome — Chest pain + fever + breathing difficulty. Life-threatening emergency. Call 112 now.
🧠 Stroke signs — Face drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty. Sickle cell patients are 300× more at risk. Call 112 immediately.
⚠️ Splenic sequestration — Sudden left-sided abdominal pain + pallor. Common in children. Urgent hospital needed.
